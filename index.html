Launch system

A simple 7-day validation sprint

  1. 1Pick one buyer

    Choose a specific audience with a repeated pain, not a vague market.

  2. 2Write the promise

    Turn the idea into one clear outcome that can be delivered quickly.

  3. 3Build a proof page

    Create a one-page offer, portfolio sample, or demo before building the full business.

  4. 4Send 30 messages

    Ask for feedback, calls, or preorders in communities where the buyer already spends time.

  5. 5Track replies

    Keep the ideas that earn clicks, replies, saves, or small payments.

  6. 6Ship the smallest version

    Deliver manually first, then automate the parts that repeat.

  7. 7Publish the result

    Share screenshots, numbers, lessons, and a new offer with a clearer angle.
